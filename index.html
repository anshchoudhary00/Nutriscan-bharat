Our prototype scale

One score.
Five easy-to-read bands.

The 0–100 health estimate is inspired by European Nutri-Score principles, UK-style nutrient traffic lights and WHO dietary guidance. It is not the official Nutri-Score, a legal food-safety rating or medical advice. Food safety requires separate evidence such as official alerts, testing and compliance checks.

0–30Very poor
31–50Poor
51–70So-so
71–89Good
90–100Excellent
The science

Food chemistry, in plain English

Ingredients have functions, and context matters. A familiar or unfamiliar chemical name by itself does not tell you whether a food is healthy or unsafe.

Carbohydrates & sugarsEnergy sources; quantity and type matter.
Protein & fibreCompare the amounts in the context of the food.
PreservativesHelp slow selected forms of spoilage when properly used.
EmulsifiersHelp ingredients such as oil and water stay mixed.
Acidity regulatorsControl acidity and can influence product stability.
AntioxidantsHelp slow oxidation in particular foods.

How NutriScan works

We use product details available from Open Food Facts, then calculate a separate educational score. The database may contain missing, user-submitted, or outdated information, so the package label remains important.

ⓘ
Not a medical diagnosisOur score is a simple comparison aid, not an official FSSAI grade or a personalised medical assessment.
⚑
Allergy information needs careDeclared allergen fields may be incomplete. Always check the current packaging, especially for serious allergies.
